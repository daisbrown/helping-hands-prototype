# Helping Hands

A friendly, accessible web demo for Latricia's personal shopping and errand service in Kings Mountain and nearby areas. Seniors, people with disabilities, and caregivers should quickly find help, explore a sample request, and call or text Latricia without an account. The phone number 704-438-5683 is approved for the demo. Service prices, operating details and request behavior are illustrative. No actual requests, payments, identity documents, or medical information are collected. Real notifications, database, email, and secure payments are later work.

The visual identity should complement her pink, heart-themed flyer and borrow the warmth and personality of Jasmine's Beets Meets Eats site. A generated illustration should depict Latricia. Big readable type, clear action labels, mobile and keyboard support, reduced motion, and visible help are priorities. The web app remains optional: calling and texting are equally valid.
